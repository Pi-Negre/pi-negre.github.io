About

Born in the Pyrenees, Pi Negre turns ideas into movies, board games, gear, and outdoor projects -- even those that might seem impossible.

  • August 2023

    Pi Negre is born in Carros de Foc

    The Carros de Foc traverse is an iconic high-mountain circular route, located in the Aigüestortes i Estany de Sant Maurici National Park in the Catalan Pyrenees. The route connects nine mountain refuges at an altitude between 1900 and 2400 meters, passing through breathtaking landscapes that include black pine forests (pi negre), over 200 glacial lakes, and rocky peaks.
